**Paper 1 in the AI Threat Hunting and Operational Security Series**

## The Problem

AI security cannot stay trapped in policy decks and prompt-injection hot takes.

The AI-Securing Threat Hunt Program is a practical operating model for finding risk across the systems organizations are already rushing to deploy.

The problem is not just *"Is the model safe?"*

The real questions are:

- What AI assets exist?
- Which tools can act on behalf of users?
- What data can prompts, agents, RAG pipelines, plugins, and vector stores access?
- Where are logs missing?
- What does abnormal behavior look like when the threat surface includes humans, models, APIs, identity, and automation?
- Can the SOC detect misuse before AI turns a normal workflow into a blast-radius event?

**AI governance without threat hunting is paperwork. AI threat hunting without governance becomes chaos.**

The program connects both: asset inventory, telemetry, hunt hypotheses, detections, response playbooks, hunter guardrails, and executive metrics.

This is blue-team AI security in the real world: not fear, not hype, not "buy a tool and pray."

**Evidence. Controls. Hunts. Response.**
